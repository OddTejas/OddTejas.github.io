Welcome weary traveller,

You stand facing the forest of mathematics, a small creek of it. Yet, it winds through the densest paths.

There is an elegance to this place. Thought the streams appear to diverge; they all belong to the same creek. Idea and logic built over centuries not bounded by a region or people. Even of the smallest scribble upholds the integrity of logic. This forest, I hope so, brings a sense of joy from the spirit of discovery, not from scripts but passed down like wisdom and folklore.

Chose a path, wonder a while...
